# Changelog

## 1.0

- First public release of KIK Trophy Desktop.
- Inspect PS5 trophy backups and transfer existing trophy histories to another local user.
- Individual and batch exports with the full user/home/<destination-UID>/trophy2/nobackup/data/<NPWR>/TRPTITLE.DAT structure.
- Validated owner-ID changes preserve every trophy record and timestamp.
- Remember valid destination IDs entered manually, loaded from another DAT or used in an export, including after app restart.
- Reject conflicting destination histories and keep opened backups unchanged.
- English default, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Italian, French, Japanese and Russian.
- Offline fonts, folder/ZIP import, clear restoration guide and verification reports.
- Portable Windows x64 build, MIT source and GitHub Actions packaging.
- Synthetic parser, persistence, batch, IPC and seven-language UI tests.
